One shape: the bird is the chat bubble

Like GitHub's mark, the whole logo is a single solid silhouette in one colour. The bird's round body is a chat bubble, its tail is the bubble's tail, and an envelope is cut out of the body. Facing left, so the tail points down and to the right, away from WhatsApp's.

1. White on the app blue

White silhouette on the blue gradient. The app icon.

2. Blue on white

The same silhouette in the brand blue on white. For websites, documents and light backgrounds.

3. White on flat blue

One flat colour, no gradient: the simplest version, like GitHub's one-colour mark.

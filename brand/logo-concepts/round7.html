MOC logo, round 7: three fresh ideas

Three different ideas: one story-driven, one the simplest possible, one a chat bubble with a twist. All in the brand blue and warm, none near WhatsApp.

1. MOC in Morse

Your name, M-O-C, written in the dashes and dots of the telegraph: the first instant mode of communication. Unique to you.

2. Growing Dots

Three dots that grow, like a message being typed: communication growing from small to big. The simplest of all.

3. Typing Bubble

An outline chat bubble holding the growing dots. Says "chat" instantly, with a twist.

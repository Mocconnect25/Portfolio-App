MOC logo, round 8: combining modes of communication

Like WhatsApp combines a message and a call, each of these combines forms of communication in one simple shape. None is near WhatsApp.

1. Letter-Bubble

An envelope that is also a chat bubble, with a new-message dot. The oldest mode of communication and the newest, in one shape.

2. Voice Bubble

A chat bubble holding a sound wave: messages and voice together. Simple, and says "we do more than text".

3. Triple Bubble

Three speech drops in a pinwheel. Abstract and ownable, like Chrome or ChatGPT: many voices, one app.

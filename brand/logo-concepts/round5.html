MOC logo, round 5: one simple shape

One bold shape each, recognisable at a glance. No bubble, no bird, no shopping.

1. The Scroll-Wave Spiral

A tiny rolled scroll (the message your old bird carried) unrolls into growing radio waves. One line, getting bolder as communication evolves. The centre dot is the sender.

2. The Ripple Ring

A glowing node sends out three bold rings through a diagonal opening. It reads as a signal, and as the O of Mode Of Communication.

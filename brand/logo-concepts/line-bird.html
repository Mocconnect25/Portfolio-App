Your bird, drawn as a line

The bird has no fill of its own: it takes the background colour, inside and outside the bubble. Only the white outline shows. The wing gap, the eye and the scroll are drawn as single clean lines.

1. Thin line

The finest, most elegant outline. Beautiful large; fades at small sizes.

2. Medium line

A balanced line. Reads well large and at 32px.

3. Bold line

The strongest line, with a thicker bubble to match. Best at small sizes.
